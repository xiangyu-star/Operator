1. choose an OSI-approved licence for original code, subject to institutional policy;
2. choose an appropriate licence for author-generated derived tables;
3. confirm that third-party repository terms permit redistribution of any included metadata or derived content; and
4. add the selected licence files to the DOI-archived release.
